Individual project · In progress (2 of 4 reviews)

Sneaky Robot RPS

A rock-paper-scissors web game where a robot tries to predict your next move. Players sign up, play, and get their results saved to a private dashboard.

Problem
Make a robot opponent that feels sneaky, while keeping accounts and saved results secure.
My decision
I built on the course's Bot Lab foundation (Flask, Flask-Login, SQLite) and added my Sneaky Robot as a Strategy function: it guesses the move I usually play after my last move and beats it. Adding it didn't change the game code.
How I checked it
I wrote my tests first, including one proving the robot never sees the player's current move. 37 tests, Black, Pylint (10/10) and GitHub Actions CI pass. I also played it in the browser, restarted the server, and confirmed rounds were still saved and private pages were blocked after sign-out.
What I learned
My test caught a database rule that only allowed the two original robots. Next time I'll list every place a new feature must be registered before coding.
Planned next
Dashboard history, account settings checks, and a classmate review.
